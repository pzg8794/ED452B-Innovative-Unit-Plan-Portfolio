# ED452B Innovative Unit Rubric Alignment

This document maps the repository and final unit plan to the ED452B Innovative Unit Plan rubric.

The rubric expects evidence across context and rationale, theory, long-term goals and standards, assessment, pedagogy, student learning analysis, unit enactment commentary, appendix evidence, target-student planning, assessment interpretation, instructional strategies, and collaboration.

## Strongest Rubric Moves

1. **Assessment does not collapse all evidence into task completion.** The unit distinguishes access, goal understanding, coding, debugging, and explanation.
2. **Target-student planning is explicit.** Supports are connected to executive-functioning, language-processing, and regulation profiles.
3. **Placement evidence drives revision.** Transcript patterns are used to redesign instruction.
4. **Appendix artifacts are purposeful.** Materials are organized around what the rubric asks for, not as random extras.
5. **The unit is honest about evidence limits.** It avoids unsupported numerical claims and instead reports recurring patterns from documented lesson evidence.
