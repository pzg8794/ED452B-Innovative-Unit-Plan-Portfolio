# Part 3 - Reflection Presentation

This section documents the final reflection / presentation component of the ED452B Innovative Unit Plan work.

## Purpose

Part 3 explains the learning behind the unit plan and communicates the most important instructional insights from the design and revision process.

The reflection should address:

- strategies used in the unit;
- what was learned while developing the unit plan;
- how placement evidence shaped revisions;
- what inclusive computer science instruction requires;
- how future teaching would change.

## Core Reflection Claim

The unit showed that a lesson can be engaging and still inaccessible if the teacher does not intentionally reduce barriers. Minecraft motivated students, but students still needed support with entry routines, NPC directions, task-goal interpretation, debugging, and collaboration roles.

## Recommended 3-Minute Presentation Flow

1. **Opening claim:** Students were not only struggling with coding; they were often struggling to access the coding task.
2. **Unit overview:** Four Grade 4 Minecraft Education lessons on algorithms, sequencing, debugging, loops, and efficiency.
3. **Inclusive strategies:** click paths, NPC read-aloud, Stop-Read-Predict-Run-Debug, Driver/Navigator roles, planning sheets, oral explanations.
4. **Evidence insight:** transcript patterns showed confusion around directions, dual-plate goals, Agent movement, and debugging routines.
5. **Professional learning:** assessment must ask what the task is really measuring.
6. **Future revision:** collect stronger artifacts, keep visible routines, and conference more systematically with target students.
